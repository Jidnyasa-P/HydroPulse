Research roadmap

Where HydroPulse stands, what limits it today, and the specific innovation that closes each gap. Everything here can be tested for free first, in the simulator or on public data.

AreaGap or limitation todayInnovation to close itFree way to test
Real-world proofLifetime results are simulated. The only real data is a public plant's flow record, used for noise.Shadow-mode pilot: run silently on 50 to 100 real units for 90 days, compare predictions with real service events, then recalibrate the simulator-to-real gap.Same engine runs on public RO data with the CSV importer.
Measurement noiseA flow-only signal leaves the failure day uncertain by about 10 days.Fuse independent signals in one filter: output-TDS salt passage, pump current, and the fill-curve shape against tank level. Independent noise averages down.Add the channels to the simulator first.
Other filtersSediment, carbon and UV come from usage only. Spent-carbon detection is 20 to 30%.Pump-current proxy for sediment clogging; Bayesian change-point detection against neighbouring homes for carbon; UV hours plus switching cycles on a Weibull curve; optional low-cost UV photodiode.Simulator plus public change-point tests.
Few neighboursThe water-event rule needs enough homes in an area.Pool information by pincode with hierarchical Bayesian priors, weather and public supply schedules.Sweep homes-per-area in the simulator.
DriftRanges were calibrated once.Adaptive conformal calibration and drift alarms (Page-Hinkley), with recalibration approved by a person.Replay rolling windows.
Fixed policyThe warning day is one number.Cost-aware decisions per home: expected cost of waiting versus replacing, using delivery time, spare stock and each customer's reaction time.Try the presets under Warning policy.
Customer experienceEnglish status screen with a basic order flow.Hindi and Marathi, WhatsApp and voice reminders, a 60-second DIY replacement video, plain "why" explanations, delivery tracking.Usability test with 5 to 10 real users.
Edge and securityRuns in the cloud. No device authentication yet.Run the small estimator on the purifier for offline use; mutual-TLS device identity, signed messages, over-the-air updates; DPDP Act 2023 consent and data minimisation.ESP32 emulator (Wokwi).
Fleet intelligencePredictions are per home only.Detect a bad cartridge batch from early failures across homes, forecast spare parts per area, batch technician routes, forecast warranty cost.Simulate batch defects and test detection.
Public goodData stays inside the product.A privacy-preserving water-quality map by area for resident associations and municipalities, with no household identifiers.Aggregation demo on the simulated fleet.
SustainabilityNot measured.Report filters saved or wasted, reject-water share and energy; advise better recovery settings.Add counters to the benchmark.
IntegrationStandalone.SDK for the Atomberg Home App and smart-home hooks (Matter, voice assistants).Mock SDK.

Highest value next: a shadow-mode pilot (closes the biggest gap: real proof), then added signal channels (shrinks the noise limit), then batch-quality detection (a new benefit for Atomberg beyond filters).
