Filter intelligence for connected purifiersConnecting

Your water filter can tell you when it needs changing.

Most purifiers count litres and guess. HydroPulse watches how the filter is really ageing and warns you weeks before it fails, so nothing breaks and nothing is thrown away too early.

1It listens

It uses what the purifier already measures. No new parts.

2It tells water from filter

Salty water or a hot day is not a failing filter.

3It warns you early

You get a date, in plain words, to change the filter.
